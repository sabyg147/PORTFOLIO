Tools for designers

Palette Extractor

Problem: CMF and UI designers pull colors from reference photos by eye or by hand-picking pixels. This tool reads an image and lists its dominant colors as hex codes in seconds. It runs in your browser, so images are never uploaded.


How to use it

  1. Choose a photo, moodboard image or render.
  2. Wait a moment for up to six dominant colors to appear.
  3. Click a swatch to copy its hex code into Figma, Illustrator or your CMF sheet.
